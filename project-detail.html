Emerald Court Apartments

15 Admiralty Road, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos State

About This Development

Emerald Court is a collection of 24 thoughtfully designed apartments set in the heart of Lekki Phase 1. Rising six floors with generous terrace views of the Lagos skyline, each unit is finished to international specification — Italian tiles, German kitchen systems, and smart-home ready wiring throughout.

Residents enjoy round-the-clock security, a resort-style pool, dedicated parking, and 24/7 power supply. The estate is gated and managed by a professional facilities team.
