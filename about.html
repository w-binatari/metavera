Our Story

Built on Purpose, Driven by People

Discover the people, principles, and passion behind Nigeria's most trusted real estate brand.

Our Founding

Started With a Simple Question: Why Should Quality Housing Be Rare?

Metavera Ltd was founded in 2016 by a group of Nigerian property professionals who had spent years watching clients get shortchanged — by unclear titles, poor construction quality, and developers who disappeared after the cheque cleared.

We set out to build something different. A company anchored in radical transparency: clear documentation, honest timelines, and homes that look better after handover than the brochure promised.

Today, Metavera has delivered over 38 projects across Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Port Harcourt, and beyond — each one a statement that quality and affordability are not opposites.

Our Mission

To democratise access to quality real estate for every Nigerian.

We exist to break the myth that premium property is only for the ultra-wealthy — delivering verified, quality-built homes across a range of budgets, backed by world-class service from first contact to key collection.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's most respected real estate brand by 2030.

Not the largest, not the loudest — the most respected. We will earn this through consistent delivery, transparent business practices, and a genuine commitment to the communities we build in.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

01

Integrity First

We say what we mean and do what we say. Every title is clean, every timeline is honest, and every contract is fair. Our reputation is more valuable than any single deal.

02

Excellence in Craft

We hold ourselves and our contractors to an uncompromising standard. Good enough is never good enough at Metavera — we build to last decades, not to look good in photos.

03

Client-Centred Always

Your investment is personal. We treat it that way. Every client — whether buying a ₦12M plot or a ₦250M penthouse — receives dedicated, personalised attention.

04

Community Impact

We don't just build buildings — we build neighbourhoods. Every Metavera project is designed to contribute positively to its surrounding community through landscaping, local hiring, and civic infrastructure.

05

Innovation & Adaptation

The real estate landscape evolves — and so do we. From diaspora payment plans to smart-home integrations, we continuously evolve our offering to match the needs of modern Nigerians.

06

Long-Term Thinking

We build for the next 30 years, not the next quarter. Every Metavera property is designed with appreciation and longevity in mind — for our clients and the communities they join.
